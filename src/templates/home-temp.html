FAQ

What is Assured Bookings

Assured Bookings is a community event booking website that consolidates a diverse range of events in one convenient location, streamlining the booking process. Users can reserve different types of events, such as exhibitions, musical performances, and private gatherings. Additionally, Assured Bookings offers a variety of services that benefit all customers when booking an event.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

You may cancel an event at any time before it occurs. If you cancel the event more than 40 days before the booking date, there will be no cancellation fee. If you cancel between 25 and 39 days before the booking date, a 40% cancellation fee will be charged. For cancellations made within 25 days of the booking date, a 100% cancellation fee will apply.

What if the event is cancelled?

In an unlikely situation of an event been cancelled, a full refund will be given to participants of the event.

How do events work?

Events on Assured Bookings are in two categories: Standard and Multi-days.

• Multi-days events run on multiple days and each day will incur part of the total event price. Users will be allowed to book more days of such an event.
• Standard events run only on a single day and a total price is paid before the event.

What if an event Availability is full?

In a situation where an event is full, Users will be allowed to join a Waiting List and if a space becomes available, the user will be offered a space for the said event.
